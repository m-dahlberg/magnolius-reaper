UPWARD COMPRESSOR

It raises what is BELOW the threshold and leaves what is above it
alone - the mirror image of an ordinary compressor. A downward
compressor turns the peaks down; this turns the quiet parts up. The
loud parts keep their level and their transients, and only the
low-level detail moves: breaths, room, the tail of a reverb, the
quiet half of a performance.

Zero latency. No lookahead, no PDC. Mix at 0% is an exact bypass.


GETTING SET UP

1. Set Range to about 6 dB first. It is the ceiling on the lift and
   the one control that stops a silent bar being dragged all the way
   up to the threshold.
2. Put Threshold under the body of the performance and above the
   noise. Everything below it gets lifted.
3. Raise Noise Floor until the pauses stop breathing. Hiss and room
   tone then stay where they are.
4. Only then reach for Ratio, Attack and Release.


ATTACK AND RELEASE ARE MIRRORED TOO

This is the one thing that trips people up.

  Attack   how fast the lift comes IN as the signal gets QUIETER
  Release  how fast it backs OFF as the signal gets louder again

So a fast attack here means quiet detail jumps up quickly, which is
the setting that sounds pumpy. That is the opposite intuition from a
downward compressor.


COMPRESSION

  Threshold    Everything below this gets lifted.
  Ratio        How much of the distance below the threshold is made
               up. 1:2 halves it. 1:1 is a bit-exact bypass.
  Range        Hard ceiling on the lift, 0 to 12 dB. The single
               most important safety control.
  Knee         Width of the soft knee straddling the threshold.
  Noise Floor  The lift fades to zero over the 6 dB below this.
  Lift         Read-only: the lift being applied right now.

DETECTOR

  Attack       Lift in, as the signal drops. Fast = pumpy.
  Release      Lift out, as the signal returns.
  SC HPF       Detector-only high pass. Raise it so bass does not
               govern how much lift the whole signal gets.
  Stereo Link  100% keeps the stereo image locked; 0% lets each
               channel act alone.
  Detector     RMS (30 ms) follows loudness, Peak (20 ms release)
               follows transients.
  Det Level    Read-only: the level the curve is being fed.

OUTPUT

  Makeup       Output gain on the WET path only.
  Mix          Wet/dry blend. 0% is an exact bypass, whatever
               Makeup is set to.
  History      Time span of the scrolling display. Cosmetic.
  Out Peak     Read-only: peak level leaving the plugin.


READING THE DISPLAY

The scrolling plot is on a -78 dB scale, mirrored about the centre
line, newest at the right edge.

  grey     the input
  cyan     the output - cyan standing outside grey is the lift
  green    the lift itself, overlaid from the bottom of the plot
           on a fixed 0 .. 12 dB scale - the top of the plot is
           12 dB, the most Range will allow. The scale never moves,
           so Range shows up as the height the green stops at
  orange   the threshold
  dotted   the noise floor

Each drawn column is the MAXIMUM over every history slot it covers,
so a transient cannot fall between pixels and make the display
disagree with your ears.

The CURVE panel is the live transfer curve, -80 .. 0 dB in. It plots
what actually leaves the plugin, Mix and Makeup included, against the
grey unity line. Watch it while you move Ratio and Knee.


WHEN SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

Nothing happens at all
  Check Range is not at 0, and Mix is not at 0%. Set Ratio to 1:1
  and the plugin is meant to be completely silent - that is a
  bit-exact bypass, not "close enough".

The quiet parts breathe or pump
  Slow the Attack down, or raise the Noise Floor until the pauses
  stop moving. Range too high does this too.

The lift meter sits at Range and never moves
  The threshold is above the whole performance. Bring it down.

Bass governs everything
  Raise SC HPF so the detector stops hearing it.

"images failed to load" in the header
  The gui_kit folder is not next to the plugin as REAPER sees it.
  If the .jsfx is symlinked into the Effects folder, gui_kit has to
  be reachable there too.

The interface is clipped
  A JSFX cannot resize its own window. Pick Fit in the header
  dropdown, or make the FX window bigger and pick 100%.
