VOCAL RIDER

Rides the vocal's gain toward a target level, smoothly, the way an
engineer rides a fader. It is gentler than a compressor: the result
sounds like an even performance, not a compressed one. Optionally it
listens to the music on a sidechain and raises the target when the
music gets loud.

The Target can be automated. The Loudness Envelope script writes a
phrase-level Target lane; the rider then only has to bring the vocal
onto that lane smoothly.

Every knob: drag up/down, Shift drags finely, the wheel nudges and
Ctrl-click resets. Click a value field to type a number (Enter
commits, Esc cancels), or drag it like a knob.


READING THE DISPLAY

Time runs right to left: the newest audio is at the cyan line on the
right. Choose 4, 8, 16 or 32 s with History.

Upper plot, in dBFS from -60 at the bottom to 0 at the top:
  Cyan, dim      peak level of the vocal input
  Cyan, bright   the vocal's loudness as the rider measures it
                 (Vocal window). This is what it compares with the
                 Target.
  Yellow line    the Target as it really is: the knob or lane, plus
                 the sidechain push
  Grey line      the sidechain (music) level
  Dashed line    the Noise floor
  Grey band      HOLD: the vocal was under the noise floor, so the
                 gain was held
  Blue band      a breath the breath guard caught

GAIN lane, below: the gain the rider applied. Up is a boost, down a
cut. Orange while riding, grey while held. The scale is +-6, 12 or
24 dB, whichever covers Max boost and Max cut.

Everything in the display is drawn after Lookahead, so the gain lines
up with the audio it was applied to.

Readouts: Target (now), Gain (now), In and Out (vocal loudness before
and after the rider, including Trim) and SC (sidechain loudness).
RIDE, HOLD and BREATH light up for what the rider is doing now.


DETECTION

Vocal window   RMS window of the vocal level detector. Shorter is
               snappier, longer smoother. Loudness Envelope measures
               with the same window.
Noise floor    Below this level the rider stops riding and holds its
               gain, so a phrase starts where the last one ended. It
               closes 150 ms after the level has dropped 3 dB under
               the floor. After 2 s of gap a boost releases toward
               0 dB, so long pauses do not lift room noise; a cut is
               held.
Lookahead      Delays the audio and reports it to REAPER as latency,
               so the gain moves before a loud word instead of after
               it. Set and forget: changing it re-syncs the track.
               0 = no latency. Leave at 0 while tracking.


RIDE

Target         The loudness the rider steers the vocal toward.
               Automate it, or let Loudness Envelope write the lane.
Amount         How much of the distance to the Target is corrected.
               100 % lands on it; 50 % goes halfway, so the vocal
               keeps half of its own phrasing.
Speed          How quickly the gain moves. Low follows word by word,
               high phrase by phrase. Cuts react a little faster
               than boosts, to avoid pumping.
Max boost      The most the rider may raise the vocal.
Max cut        The most the rider may lower it. Typing -6 or 6 both
               give 6 dB of cut.
Tail lift      How much the rider may raise the gain while the
               level is falling: the end of a long note, or the
               softer last words of a phrase. 100 % lifts them like
               any quiet part, 0 % leaves those fades as sung.


BREATH

Threshold      How noise-like a quiet frame must be to count as a
               breath (energy above 3 kHz minus energy below
               1.5 kHz, in dB). Lower catches more. Watch the blue
               bands and the BREATH light while you set it.
Guard          What happens to a breath. In every mode but Off, a
               breath gets no say in the gain.
                 Freeze    the gain stays where it was when the
                           breath began
                 No boost  as Freeze, but a breath after a boosted
                           note is left at 0 dB
               Neither mode turns a breath down below 0 dB: this is
               not a de-breather.


SIDECHAIN & OUTPUT

Influence      How many dB the Target rises when the music is loud.
               The full amount applies when the music is 12 dB above
               the Target. 0 = off.
SC window      RMS window of the sidechain detector.
Trim           Fixed gain after the rider.

To feed the sidechain: set the vocal track to 4 channels, send the
music to it on channels 3/4, and check the plugin's pins map
Sidechain L/R to 3/4. The sidechain is never heard.


RIDING A LOUDNESS ENVELOPE LANE

Amount 100 %, Speed 200-400 ms, Lookahead 100-200 ms for mixing, and
Max boost / Max cut at least the level difference between the takes
plus the ride you want.


WHEN SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

Always HOLD         the Noise floor is above the vocal. Lower it
                    until the cyan body sits above the dashed line.
Gain pinned at a    Max boost or Max cut is too small for the
limit               distance to the Target.
Breaths lifted      set Guard to No boost, or lower Threshold until
                    the breaths show blue.
Phrase ends lifted  lower Tail lift.
Nothing moves       the window is empty: audio is not playing
                    through the plugin.
"images failed to   the gui_kit folder is missing next to the
load" in the header plugin file.
