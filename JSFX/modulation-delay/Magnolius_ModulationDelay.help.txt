MOD DELAY

A stereo delay with three characters, Digital, BBD and Tape, from one
engine. The character comes from what happens inside the feedback
loop: the echo gets darker, thinner and a little more smeared, and the
colour compounds with every pass. The modulation moves the read
position inside the loop, so every repeat drifts a little further
than the one before.

Every knob: drag up/down, Shift drags finely, the wheel nudges and
Ctrl-click resets. Click a value field to type a number (Enter
commits, Esc cancels), or drag it like a knob.


QUICK START

The defaults are a quarter-note-ish analog echo: 375 ms, BBD, 40 %
feedback, 30 % mix. Pick Character first. Digital is a clean
reference, BBD is darker and noisier with a slow clock, Tape adds wow,
flutter, a head bump and a little warmth. Character sets Time change
(Digital crossfades, BBD and Tape glide) but nothing is locked, so you
can still build a bright BBD or a dark digital delay.

For a send, turn Wet only on: it forces 100 % wet without touching
the Mix value. The wet signal alone is also on output pins 3/4.


READING THE DISPLAY

Left, REPEATS: every repeat as a bar, L above R, over 3.2 seconds.
The first echo is always full level. Later bars fall with Feedback
and Curve, and turn from cyan to orange: orange repeats have been
through the loop more often. A bar blurs wider with Mod depth, because the
modulation compounds with every pass. In Ping-pong the repeats
alternate between the lanes. With Freeze on they all stay full.

Right, LOOP RESPONSE: what one pass through the loop filters does
(solid, the first echo) and what four passes do (dashed). In BBD the
low-pass follows the delay time, and the curve shows where it sits.


TIME

  Time          1 ms to 2 s. Greyed while Sync is on; it then shows
                the synced time. Sync follows tempo changes, including
                automation, within one block.
  R offset      Moves the right channel's time by up to +-50 %, for
                width without ping-pong.
  Cross         Only in Cross mode: how much of each loop leaks to
                the other side, 0 to 100 %.
  Glide         The time change slew, 20 to 500 ms (Glide only).
  Sync          Off, or 1/1 down to 1/32. Sync type picks straight,
                dotted or triplet. Synced times stop at 4 seconds.
  Stereo mode   Stereo: two independent loops. Ping-pong: the input
                is summed to mono and the repeats alternate sides.
                Cross: independent loops with a bleed (Cross knob).
  Time change   Glide: the read position slews, so a time change
                bends pitch like tape. Crossfade: a second read head
                fades in over 30 ms with no pitch change. A change
                that arrives mid-fade waits for it to end.


LOOP

The first echo and every repeat after it go through the same chain,
in a fixed order: filters, saturation, smear. What goes round again
is that signal times the feedback gain, so the colour compounds with
every pass: echo 1 has been through the chain once, echo 2 twice.

  Feedback      0 to 110 %. The first echo always plays at full
                level. Feedback sets the NUMBER of repeats: how
                many it takes to fall 60 dB (about 3 repeats at
                10 %, 7.5 at 40 %, 10 at 50 %, 45 at 85 %). Above
                100 % the loop saturates instead of running away.
  Curve         The shape of the fall, -100 to +100 %. 0 is the
                plain feedback: the same drop on every repeat.
                Positive holds the early repeats up and then drops
                them faster. Negative drops the first repeats
                quickly and leaves a long quiet tail. The repeat at
                the -60 dB point is the same for every curve, so
                Feedback still sets how long it lasts.
  HPF / LPF     Thinner and darker. HPF 20 Hz-5 kHz, LPF 200 Hz-
                20 kHz. Each is exactly off at the end of its range
                (HPF 20 Hz, LPF 20 kHz). The slope boxes pick 6 or
                12 dB/oct; 6 compounds more gently over many
                repeats.
  Drive         Soft clipping in the loop. At 0 it is clean, but a
                hard ceiling at about +6 dBFS still holds, so a
                runaway feedback becomes a sustained wash.
  Smear         0 to 4 short all-pass stages that blur transients a
                little more on each pass. It is continuous: 2.5
                is two full stages and a half-faded third.
  Freeze        Sets feedback to 100 %, mutes new input into the
                loop and bypasses the loop filters, so the frozen
                material holds its tone.

A DC blocker is always on inside the loop (after the saturation).


MODULATION

Moves the read position of each delay line, inside the loop.

  Rate          0.05 to 10 Hz.
  Depth         0 to 10 ms. The panel header shows the resulting
                peak pitch deviation in cents.
  Phase         0 to 180 degrees between the left and right LFOs.
  Shape         Sine, Triangle, or smoothed Random.
  Depth scaling Fixed: depth in ms. Relative: depth grows with the
                delay time (depth x time / 250 ms).
  Wow, Flutter  Tape only (the knobs are grey otherwise). Wow is a
                slow drift around 0.3-2 Hz, flutter a fast one at
                6-20 Hz. They add to the main LFO.

Every modulation source is smoothed, so moving a control never steps
the read position. The read can never get closer to the write than 4
samples.


OUTPUT

Applied to the wet signal after the loop, so these never change how
the repeats evolve.

  Duck          How far the wet signal is pulled down while the dry
                input is above Threshold. The wet swells back in the
                gaps over Release (50 to 1000 ms). Attack is 10 ms.
  Width         M/S width of the wet signal only, 0 to 150 %.
  Mix           Equal-power: no level jump at 50 %.
  Output        +-12 dB trim on everything.
  Wet only      100 % wet, for sends. The Mix value is untouched.
  CLEAR         Empties the delay. It also happens when you change
                Character, start playback, seek, or loop back.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

  "images failed to load" in the header
        The gui_kit folder is not beside the plugin. Reinstall.
  A repeat is missing right after a seek
        That is the clear on seek: nothing stale is replayed.
  The delay time does not follow the Time knob
        Sync is on. Turn Sync off.
  Wow and Flutter do nothing
        They are Tape only.
